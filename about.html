About

A US-based supplier, built for research buyers.

Glow Research supplies high-purity research peptides to laboratories, institutions, and qualified researchers across the United States. Everything we sell is intended for in-vitro laboratory research use only.

We are not a manufacturer, and we do not pretend to be one. We are a US-based retailer that partners with a domestic, cGMP-aligned production facility, and we third-party test what we sell.

Where we sit in the chain

Most peptide sites are vague about this on purpose. We would rather be direct: Glow Research is the retailer. Synthesis and fill happen at a US-based partner facility that operates to cGMP-aligned quality practices, and our role is sourcing, qualifying, verifying, and shipping.

That distinction matters when you are evaluating a supplier, because it tells you exactly which claims are ours to make and which belong to the facility behind the vial.

Why the supply chain stays domestic

Production, fill, storage, and fulfillment all happen inside the United States. Keeping the chain domestic means shorter transit, fewer temperature excursions in transit, and a paper trail we can actually follow back to a specific lot when a customer asks.

It also means we are not reconciling documentation across jurisdictions when you need a certificate for a specific batch.

Verification, not adjectives

Every lot is tested by an independent third-party laboratory, and every vial carries a lot number that maps to its certificate of analysis. If you want to see a lot's COA before you order rather than after, email us and we will send it.

Purity claims that are not attached to a document are marketing. We try to keep ours attached to one.

Research use, enforced

Our products are not drugs, supplements, foods, or cosmetics. They are not for human or animal consumption, and we do not sell to anyone purchasing for personal use.

We operate under the applicable federal, state, and local requirements for research chemical distribution, and we reserve the right to refuse any order that falls outside a legitimate research context.
