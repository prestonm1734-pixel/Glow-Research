About

A San Diego supplier,
built for research buyers.

Glow Research is a San Diego, California supplier of research peptides, selling to laboratories and qualified researchers nationwide.

We are not a manufacturer, and we do not pretend to be one. Production runs at a U.S. cGMP-aligned partner facility, and analysis is done by an independent third-party laboratory.

Where we sit in the chain

Glow Research is the retailer, and only the retailer. We do not manufacture, compound, repackage, or test.

Read details

Most peptide sites are vague about this on purpose. Synthesis and fill happen at a US-based partner facility that operates to cGMP-aligned quality practices. Analysis is performed by an independent third-party laboratory. Orders are picked, packed, and dispatched by a third-party fulfilment partner. We do not operate a laboratory or a warehouse. Our role is peptide sourcing: qualifying the suppliers behind each lot, and putting their documentation in front of you.

That distinction matters when you are evaluating a supplier, because it tells you exactly which claims are ours to make and which belong to the facility behind the vial. The whole chain, step by step, is on our process page.

Why the supply chain stays domestic

Production, fill, storage, and fulfilment all happen inside the United States, at our partners’ facilities rather than ours.

Read details

Keeping the chain domestic means shorter transit, fewer temperature excursions in transit, and a paper trail we can actually follow back to a specific lot when a customer asks.

It also means we are not reconciling documentation across jurisdictions when you need a certificate for a specific batch, and that dispatch is measured in hours rather than customs windows: orders placed before 2:00 PM PST get same-day shipping on 2-day FedEx Express.

Verification, not adjectives

Every lot is tested by an independent third-party laboratory, and every batch has its own certificate of analysis.

Read details

HPLC purity and mass-spec identity, issued against the lot number printed on the vial. Email us with a lot number, before you order or after, and we will send the certificate for that exact batch.

Purity claims that are not attached to a document are marketing. We try to keep ours attached to one.

Research use, enforced

Research compounds only: not drugs, supplements, foods, or cosmetics, and never for human or animal consumption.

Read details

We do not sell to anyone purchasing for personal use. We operate under the applicable federal, state, and local requirements for research chemical distribution, and we reserve the right to refuse any order that falls outside a legitimate research context.

The standard

Five rules we do not trade away.

Every supplier says it cares about quality. These five are written into the codebase that builds this site, and they settle arguments about what ships and what does not.

01

Never create uncertainty we can remove.

Read details

If a customer would have to guess about stock, about a lot, about when something ships or what happens next, that is a defect, and we treat it as one. Ambiguity is not neutral. It is the thing that sends someone back to comparing suppliers.

02

Never claim more than we can prove.

Read details

Every factual statement on this site has to trace back to something the business actually holds: a document, a measured figure, a policy we follow. Where the evidence is not in hand yet, we say what is true today and route you to the person who can send you the rest. We would rather be quiet than impressive.

03

Consistency is part of the product.

Read details

Order number ten and order number one hundred thousand should feel like they came from the same company: the same terminology, the same packaging, the same documentation, the same answer to the same question. A supplier you have to re-evaluate on every order is a supplier you are still shopping around.

04

Problems should improve the system.

Read details

When something goes wrong, fixing the individual order is the minimum, not the resolution. The failure has to change a process so the same thing cannot reach the next customer. A complaint that produces an apology and nothing else has been wasted.

05

Growth cannot lower the Glow standard.

Read details

Anything that makes us faster or more profitable while making the experience less dependable is treated as suspect, no matter what it does to the numbers. Scale is not a reason for a supplier to get worse, and it is the most common one.

What we judge everything against Would this make an existing Glow customer more likely or less likely to ever need another supplier?

Everyone else in this category competes for the order. We would rather end the search, so that comparing suppliers stops being something you think about.

Never let growth turn Glow into the company we built Glow to replace.
