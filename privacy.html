Privacy Policy

What we collect, what we don't, and where your information actually lives.

Last updated August 2026

This policy covers glowresearch.shop, operated by Glow Nutrition LLC. It describes what information the site collects, how it's used, and what's stored only in your own browser rather than sent to us.

1. Information You Provide

If you use the contact form, sign-up form, or checkout, you may give us your name, email address, phone number, shipping and billing address, and company name. We use this to respond to inquiries, process orders, and ship products.

Accounts and checkout are not connected to a live backend yet. Nothing typed into the sign-in or checkout forms today is transmitted, stored on a server, or seen by anyone — the password field in particular is never read at all. This policy will be updated with specifics once the account system and payment processor go live, and we'll note the change here rather than update it quietly.

2. Information Stored in Your Browser

Glow Research uses your browser's local storage, not tracking cookies, to remember a small set of functional details on the device you're using:

  • Cart contents — what you've added, so it's still there if you reload the page.
  • Age and RUO gate confirmation — so the entry screen doesn't reappear on every visit.
  • Referral code — if you arrived through a partner link, which partner should be credited.
  • Sample sign-in state — while the account system is a design preview, this is a placeholder value, not real authentication.

This information stays on your device. We do not receive it, sell it, or share it with anyone. Clearing your browser's site data for glowresearch.shop removes all of it.

3. What We Don't Do

Glow Research does not run third-party analytics, advertising pixels, or behavioral tracking scripts. We don't sell or rent personal information to anyone, and we don't build advertising profiles from your activity on this site.

4. Third-Party Services

A small number of outside services support the site:

  • Google Fonts — typefaces are loaded from Google's servers, which means your browser makes a request to Google when the page loads.
  • Fulfilment partner — once an order is placed, your shipping details are shared with the third-party partner who picks, packs, and dispatches it on our behalf. See Terms & Conditions for how that relationship works.
  • Payment processor — when checkout is connected to a live payment gateway, card details will be handled directly by that processor. Glow Research does not store full card numbers.

5. Cookies

This site does not set tracking or advertising cookies. Any first-party cookie a future payment or hosting provider requires for the checkout to function will be disclosed here when checkout goes live.

6. Your Choices

You can clear everything the site has stored in your browser at any time through your browser's settings — this removes your cart, age-gate confirmation, and referral code. To ask about, correct, or delete information tied to an order, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

Glow Research products are restricted to buyers 21 and older, and this site is not directed at anyone under that age. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information you provide to us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security for information sent over the internet.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the site changes, particularly once accounts and payment processing go live. Continued use of the site after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or a request about your information can be sent to support@glowresearch.shop, or by mail to Glow Nutrition LLC, 10755 Scripps Poway Pkwy #376, San Diego, CA 92131.
