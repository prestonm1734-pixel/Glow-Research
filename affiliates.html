Affiliate program FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much do I earn?

20% of the order subtotal on every order placed through your link. Shipping and taxes are excluded, and the rate is the same on every product.

How do I get paid?

By PayPal, Venmo, Cash App or bank transfer. You choose your method in the affiliate dashboard, and your earnings are tracked there in real time.

How long is the referral cookie?

30 days. If someone clicks your link and orders within 30 days, the order is credited to you.

Which products can I promote?

The whole catalog. Every compound has a published certificate for its current lot, so you can link straight to the proof.

Can I refer myself?

No. Orders you place through your own link are not recorded, and neither are orders from other partners.

What happens if a referred order is refunded?

Refunded or cancelled orders do not earn commission. Sales are verified automatically against the order status.

Is there a minimum audience?

No. Sign-up is free and approval is instant, whatever the size of your following.
