How to read a certificate.

Each certificate reports three analyses on one lot. Match the lot number on your vial to the one on the certificate, and every figure below is yours.

Purity

Measured by HPLC: the share of the sample that is the compound itself, as a percentage.

Identity

Confirms the compound in the vial is the one named on the label.

Quantity

The amount actually measured in the vial, set against the amount on the label.

Lot and report

The lot number ties the certificate to your vial, and the report reference can be checked against the laboratory's own records.
