How it works

Concentration

Mass divided by diluent volume: 5 mg / 2 mL = 2.5 mg/mL. Adding diluent does not change how much compound is in the vial, only how much liquid it is spread through.

Draw volume

Target amount divided by concentration. Because the target is in micrograms, the concentration is converted first: 250 mcg / 2500 mcg/mL = 0.1 mL.

Syringe units

A U-100 insulin syringe is marked in hundredths of a millilitre, so 1 mL = 100 units. The unit figure is just the draw volume multiplied by 100.

Aliquots per vial

Total mass divided by the target amount, rounded down. Reconstituted material has a much shorter working life than the lyophilized powder, so plan the whole vial before you open it.
