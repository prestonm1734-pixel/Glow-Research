How it works

Concentration

Mass divided by diluent volume: 5 mg / 2 mL = 2.5 mg/mL. Adding diluent does not change how much compound is in the vial, only how much liquid it is spread through.

Net peptide content

The vial's stated mass includes counterions and residual moisture, so actual peptide content can run lower than the label figure. Check your certificate of analysis for a net content value where one is reported.

Shelf life after reconstitution

Reconstituted material has a much shorter working life than the lyophilized powder. Refrigerate, protect from light, and do not refreeze a thawed solution.
