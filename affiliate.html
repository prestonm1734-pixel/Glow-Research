How it works

From click to payout.

No dashboard gymnastics. A link, a window, a monthly transfer.

  1. You get a code and a link

    On approval your account issues a code — GLOW-XXXXXX — and a link in the form glowresearch.shop/?ref=GLOW-XXXXXX. Point it at any page on the site; the code travels regardless of where it lands.

  2. The click is credited for 60 days

    We record the code in the visitor's browser and strip it back out of the address bar. Any order they place in the next 60 days is credited to you, on any device where that browser session persists. Last click wins if a buyer arrives through more than one affiliate.

  3. Commission clears after the return window

    Commission is calculated on the order subtotal, before shipping and tax. It sits as pending for 30 days after delivery so refunds and chargebacks can settle, then moves to approved.

  4. Paid monthly, once you clear $50

    Approved commission is paid on the 1st of the month to PayPal or by ACH, once the balance passes $50. Below that it rolls into the next month. US affiliates paid $600 or more in a year get a 1099-NEC, so a W-9 is collected at approval.
