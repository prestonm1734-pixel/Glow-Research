  1. 01

    U.S. Manufacturing Partner

    Synthesized and filled at a U.S. partner facility operating to cGMP-aligned quality practices.

    Performed by our manufacturing partner
    Details

    A domestic chain is a shorter one: fewer hands, and a paper trail that stays inside one jurisdiction when you ask us to trace a lot back.

  2. 02

    Independent Testing

    Each lot is analyzed by an independent laboratory: identity by LC-MS, purity by HPLC-UV, and an endotoxin screen by LAL assay.

    Performed by an independent laboratory
    Details

    HPLC-UV for purity, LC-MS for identity, and endotoxin testing by LAL assay under USP chapter 85, performed by a laboratory with no stake in the result. The laboratory issues the certificate, not us. We are not qualified to grade our own inventory and we do not try to.

    Sterility is not part of this panel. An endotoxin pass is not a sterility result, and we do not represent it as one.

  3. 03

    Batch Documentation

    Each batch has documentation tied to the lot number printed on the vial.

    Issued by the testing laboratory
    Details

    A certificate is only worth something if you can match it to the vial in front of you. Every batch has its own, issued against the lot number printed on the label, not a representative document for the compound in general.

    Email support@glowresearch.shop with the compound and lot number, or the order number if you have already bought, and we will send the certificate for that exact batch, including batches that have since sold out.

  4. 04

    Controlled Handling

    Received, stored, and held by lot in a climate-controlled U.S. fulfillment facility.

    Performed by our fulfillment partner
    Details

    Holding stock by lot is what keeps the batch a vial belongs to out of question once it is on the shelf. Every hour material spends outside a controlled environment is an hour nobody can document.

  5. 05

    Lot Assignment

    Each order is assigned to a specific lot so the vial and its documentation stay connected.

    Performed by Glow, with our fulfillment partner
    Details

    That number travels with the vial: it is on the label you receive, and it is what we look up when you write in. Ask for the certificate on a lot you already have, months later, and there is something to look up.

  6. 06

    Dispatch

    Orders placed before 2:00 PM PST leave the same business day.

    Performed by our fulfillment partner
    Details

    Picked and sealed before the FedEx cutoff, then 2-day FedEx Express anywhere in the United States, Hawaii and Alaska included. Tracking lands within 24 hours, and every order ships the same way. There is no cheaper tier that skips ahead of yours.
