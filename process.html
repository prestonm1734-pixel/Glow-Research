The chain

Where a vial comes from, in order.

Every step below is either something we do or something we can name the party for. Nothing on this page describes a capability Glow owns.

01

U.S. Manufacturing Partner

Synthesized and filled at a U.S. partner facility operating to cGMP-aligned quality practices.

Performed by our manufacturing partner
Read details

Keeping production domestic is what makes the rest of this page possible: a shorter chain, fewer hands, and a paper trail that stays inside one jurisdiction when you ask us to trace a lot back.

02

Independent Batch Testing

Each lot is analyzed by an independent laboratory using HPLC for purity and mass spectrometry for identity.

Performed by an independent laboratory
Read details

HPLC for purity, mass spectrometry for identity, performed by a laboratory with no stake in the result. The distinction that matters: the lab issues the certificate, not us. We are not qualified to grade our own inventory and we do not try to.

03

Batch Documentation

Each batch has documentation tied to the lot number printed on the vial.

Issued by the testing laboratory
Read details

A certificate is only worth anything if you can match it to the vial in front of you. Every batch has its own, issued against the lot number printed on the label, not a representative document for the compound in general, and not a screenshot of a good result from a different run.

Email support@glowresearch.shop with the compound and lot number, or the order number if you have already bought, and we will send the certificate for that exact batch, including batches that have since sold out.

04

Controlled Handling

Received, stored, and held by lot in a climate-controlled U.S. fulfillment facility.

Performed by our fulfillment partner
Read details

Holding stock by lot is what keeps the batch a vial belongs to out of question once it is on the shelf. Lyophilized material is stable, but the reason we keep the chain domestic is that every hour it is not in a controlled environment is an hour nobody can document.

05

Order Processing

Each order is assigned to a specific lot so the vial and its documentation stay connected.

Performed by Glow, with our fulfillment partner
Read details

That lot number travels with the vial: it is on the label you receive, and it is what we look up when you write in. That is the whole reason the number exists. Ask us for the certificate on a lot you already have, months later, and there is something to look up.

06

Dispatch

Orders placed before 2:00 PM PST leave the same business day.

Performed by our fulfillment partner
Read details

Picked, sealed, and out the door before the FedEx cutoff, then 2-day FedEx Express anywhere in the United States, Hawaii and Alaska included. Tracking lands within 24 hours. Free over $400, and every order ships the same way. There is no cheaper tier that skips ahead of yours.
