Quality and testing

Three tests. Every batch.

Every lot is tested by an independent laboratory before it reaches our catalog. No exceptions.

HPLC for purity, an identity check against the declared compound, a quantity assay reporting the milligrams weighed out of the vial, and lot archival linking every batch to its certificate.

Our partners

We do not run any of these tests.

Glow does not manufacture and does not operate a laboratory. Testing is Accumark Labs, an independent lab with no stake in the result, and the certificate is issued in their name. Their ISO/IEC 17025 accreditation is pending, not granted, so read their reports as the work of an independent laboratory rather than an accredited one.

You do not have to take any of this from us. Every certificate carries the laboratory’s own report reference, and accumarklabs.com/verify ↗ checks it against their records. Each certificate on this site links it with the reference already filled in.
