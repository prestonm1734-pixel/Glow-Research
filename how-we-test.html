The Methods

Eight checks on every lot.

Reverse-Phase HPLC-UV

Purity quantified against reference standards. The figure printed on every certificate.

LC-MS

Molecular identity confirmed by mass-to-charge analysis: the compound matches what the label claims.

Net Peptide Content

The actual peptide mass in the vial, so dosing math starts from a real number, not a label.

Sterility

Screened for microbial growth. A clean result means nothing grew.

Endotoxin (LAL)

Bacterial endotoxin screened against a defined threshold on every lot.

Appearance & Solubility

The lyophilate and its reconstitution behavior are inspected before a lot is released.

Heavy Metals

Trace elemental contaminants screened against safe limits.
