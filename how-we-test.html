The panel

What gets checked.

Seven separate analyses, run on the lot before it is released for sale.

01

Purity

Reverse-phase HPLC-UV

How much of the powder is the peptide you ordered. This is the percentage printed on the certificate.

02

Identity

LC-MS

Weighs the molecule to confirm it is the one on the label, not something close to it.

03

Net peptide content

How many milligrams of actual peptide are in the vial once salt and water are taken out.

04

Sterility

The lot is cultured to see whether anything grows. Nothing should.

05

Endotoxin

LAL assay, USP chapter 85

Checks for bacterial toxin, which is left behind even after the bacteria themselves are gone.

06

Appearance and solubility

The powder is looked at and dissolved. It should look right and go into solution cleanly.

07

Heavy metals

Screens for lead, arsenic and the other metals that can carry over from manufacturing.

The document

Documented, not claimed.

A certificate is only worth something if it is specific. Every result is tied to the lot number printed on your vial, not to the product in general. This is the structure every one of them follows.

  • One lot number, matching the vial in your hand
  • The lab that ran it, and the date they ran it
