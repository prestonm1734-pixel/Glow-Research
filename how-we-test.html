Five tests.
Every batch.

Every batch is tested by an independent, certified laboratory before we sell it. No exceptions. Here is what they check.

What gets tested

What each test is for.

HPLC-UV for purity, LC-MS for identity, sterility testing, endotoxin testing by LAL assay under USP chapter 85, and chemical contaminant screening.

01
HPLC Purity

How much of it is the peptide

Separates the powder into its parts and measures each one. Tells you what percentage is the peptide you ordered, and what is not.

02
LC-MS Identity

Whether it is the right peptide

Weighs the molecule. Every peptide sequence has its own mass, so the result either matches the name on the label or it does not.

03
Sterility Microbiological safety

Whether anything is growing in it

Checks the batch for living microorganisms. A clean result means nothing grew.

04
Endotoxin Bacterial residue

What bacteria may have left behind

Bacteria leave fragments behind even after they die. The LAL assay, run under USP chapter 85, puts a number on how much is there.

05
Contaminants Chemical screening

What else ended up in the vial

Screens for chemicals left over from making the peptide, so what you get is the compound and nothing extra.

Who runs it

We do not test our own products.

A result we produced ourselves would prove nothing. So we do not produce one.

Glow does not manufacture, does not run a laboratory, and does not write certificates. A certified independent laboratory does the testing and signs the certificate.

Every certificate covers one batch, and that batch number is printed on your vial. Match the two and you know the document is about the vial in your hand, not a sample from last year.

Want it confirmed? Take the report number on the certificate to the laboratory that issued it. Check it with them, not with us.

Where it happens

Testing is step two of six.

The analysis above sits inside a longer chain, and four of its six links are performed by someone other than us. Each one names who.

  1. 01

    U.S. Manufacturing Partner

    Synthesized and filled at a U.S. partner facility operating to cGMP-aligned quality practices.

    Performed by our manufacturing partner
    Details

    A domestic chain is a shorter one: fewer hands, and a paper trail that stays inside one jurisdiction when you ask us to trace a lot back.

  2. 02

    Independent Testing

    Every batch is tested by an independent certified laboratory for purity, identity, sterility, endotoxin and contaminants.

    Performed by an independent laboratory
    Details

    HPLC-UV for purity, LC-MS for identity, and endotoxin testing by LAL assay under USP chapter 85, performed by a laboratory with no stake in the result. The laboratory issues the certificate, not us. We are not qualified to grade our own inventory and we do not try to.

    Sterility is not part of this panel. An endotoxin pass is not a sterility result, and we do not represent it as one.

  3. 03

    Batch Documentation

    Each batch has documentation tied to the lot number printed on the vial.

    Issued by the testing laboratory
    Details

    A certificate is only worth something if you can match it to the vial in front of you. Every batch has its own, issued against the lot number printed on the label, not a representative document for the compound in general.

  4. 04

    Controlled Handling

    Received, stored, and held by lot in a climate-controlled U.S. fulfillment facility.

    Performed by our fulfillment partner
    Details

    Holding stock by lot is what keeps the batch a vial belongs to out of question once it is on the shelf. Every hour material spends outside a controlled environment is an hour nobody can document.

  5. 05

    Each order is assigned to a specific lot so the vial and its documentation stay connected.

    Details
