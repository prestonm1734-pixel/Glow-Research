Handling

  • Store lyophilized at −20°C. Sealed and frozen, the powder is the most stable form this material comes in.
  • Keep it out of light. Leave the vial in its box until it goes into the freezer.
  • Let it reach room temperature before opening. Opening a cold vial pulls in moisture, and moisture is what degrades a lyophilized powder.
  • Record the lot number before you discard the box. It is the only way to pull the matching COA later.
  • Arrived warm or damaged? Do not use it. Send us the order number and we will replace the shipment.
